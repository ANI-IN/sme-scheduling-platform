The app runs an **automatic check** every hour. Each time, it does what is due for every class week. Messages go out within a few minutes, and a reply an instructor types by email or in their Discord channel shows up as a task within 6 minutes.

![What happens before a class](/help/timeline.png)

## The timeline (default settings)

1. **45 days before:** the setup check. Missing class times, owners or instructors are reported to the owner.
2. **30 days before:** the app asks the Primary instructor.
3. **No reply:** a reminder after a while, then the next instructor is asked. The closer the class, the shorter the wait.
4. **First Yes:** that instructor is confirmed. Everyone else asked is told politely.
5. **14 days before:** the module owner is asked if everything is ready.
6. **10 and 2 days before:** reminders before class. The instructor can answer All set, Need help, or Can't make it.

The exact days are on the Automation page and can differ per cohort.

## Close and very close to the class

- **Close to the class** (10 days by default): problems are urgent and replies are expected faster.
- **Very close to the class** (3 days by default): replies are expected fastest and quiet hours are shorter.

## Quiet hours

Instructors are not messaged at night, in the time zone of their class (US Pacific or India). Messages wait until the morning.

## On the Instructor requests page

![Instructor requests](/help/instructor-requests.png)

- Press a summary card to show only those class weeks.
- **Ask the next instructor** skips the person being asked.
- **Start asking now** asks before the usual day.
- **More** has: change the order for this class week, ask a specific person, assign an instructor, record a phone or chat confirmation, and pause requests.

> Only one request is open per class week at a time, and an instructor is never confirmed for two classes at the same time.
