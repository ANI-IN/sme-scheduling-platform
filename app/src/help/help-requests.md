Instructors can press **Need help** in a reminder before class. They choose what they need (for example the Zoom link or the class materials) and how urgent it is. Your reply always goes to their Discord channel (or a direct message) and by email.

![A help request](/help/help-request.png)

1. The request appears as a task on [Home](/) and is sent to the right people (owners, the operations team, or both, depending on the kind of request).
2. Press **Open help request**.
3. Press **I'm on it** so the instructor and the team know someone is handling it.
4. Write your answer under **Reply to the instructor** and press **Send reply**. Tick **This resolves it** when nothing more is needed.
5. If you solved it another way, write a note and press **Resolved outside the app**.

## Reply times

Each request has a time by which it should be answered. It depends on how urgent the instructor said it is, and is shorter when the class is close. Halfway through, the people it is for get a reminder; when the time passes, the escalation contacts are told.

## When help really means "I can't teach"

Press **Treat as a cancellation**. The instructor is released and the app starts asking other instructors.

> You can also add a help request yourself, for example after a phone call: open the class week and use **Add a help request**.
