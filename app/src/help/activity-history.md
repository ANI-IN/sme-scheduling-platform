[Activity history](/activity) lists everything that happened: changes, messages, answers from instructors, and what the automatic check did. Nobody can change or delete it.

![Activity history](/help/activity.png)

1. Choose **This domain** or **All domains**.
2. Narrow it down with the filters: **Type of change**, cohort, module, instructor, **Changed by**, and the **From** and **To** dates. **Clear dates** removes the dates.
3. Type a word into **Search activity**, for example a name.
4. Press **Show details** under an entry to see exactly what changed.
5. Press **Show more** at the bottom to see older entries.

Times are in your own time zone, and each one also shows the US Pacific time.

## Who did it

- A name means a person on the team did it.
- **System** means the automatic check did it.
- An instructor's name means they answered a message.
- **Database admin** means someone changed the data directly, outside the app.

Entries made while a domain was in testing show **Testing**.

## Undo

**Undo** shows only for changes that are safe to reverse, for example an edit to an instructor or an instructor order. It restores the previous values, and the entry then shows **Undone**. Creating or deleting a domain, cohort or module cannot be undone, and messages that were sent are never recalled.

## Download

Press **Download as spreadsheet** to save the list you see. Downloads are also recorded in the history.
