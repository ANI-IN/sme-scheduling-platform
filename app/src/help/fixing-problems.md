Most problems show in three places: as a badge or status on the [Schedule board](/board), as a count under **Needs a decision**, and often as a task on [Home](/). You always fix them from the class week's side panel. Press **Legend** on the board to see every badge with one sentence about it.

## Clash

**Clash** (red) means the instructor is in two classes at the same time, in any cohort, domain or region. The app compares the exact moments, so a Saturday 7:30 PM PST class and a Sunday 9:00 AM IST class are the same time.

1. On the board, select **Clash** under **Needs a decision** to see every class week with one.
2. Open the class week. The problem says which other class they teach, its cohort and both local times.
3. Fix it in one of three ways: change the time of one class with **Change time**, press **Release instructor** to find someone else, or choose another instructor.

The app never lets you create a clash: assigning a busy instructor or moving a class onto another of their classes is blocked, and a busy instructor is skipped in the instructor order with the reason shown.

## Shared class

A shared class is one class for several cohorts: one instructor and one set of messages, while each cohort keeps its own lists. The class week that keeps the class shows the others under **Cohorts sharing this class**. The others show **Shared class** and are not asked.

![Sharing a class with other cohorts](/help/share-class.png)

1. Open the class week that keeps the class and its instructor, and press **Share with other cohorts** under **Shared class**. Or select the class weeks on the board and press **Make one shared class**: the earliest one keeps the class.
2. Tick the other cohorts' class weeks. If their times differ, choose whose times the shared class keeps. Each time is checked in every cohort's time zone.
3. If another class week has its own confirmed instructor, tick **Release instructors confirmed for the other class weeks**, or share from that class week instead to keep that instructor.
4. Check the preview and press **Share class**. Sharing class weeks of different modules needs a reason.

A shared class is moved on the class week that keeps it, and the others follow. **Stop sharing** gives a cohort its own class week back at the shared time, and its instructor order is asked.

## Cohort merge

Two or more cohorts continue as one combined cohort from a merge date. Each shows **Merged into** with the date. When the merge check finds two confirmed instructors for the same module, the chosen cohort's instructor stays. To change your mind before the merge date, press **Undo merge**. See [Breaks, cohort holidays and merges](/help/breaks-and-holidays).

## Break

A cohort break moves every later class week of one cohort back by whole weeks. A program break does the same for every cohort of the domain. On the board, a week of a break shows **Break** and its reason, and a program break is one row across the board.

- A class planned during a break shows **During a break**. Move it, cancel it, or remove the break.
- A confirmed instructor who can't teach the new dates is listed when you add the break. Tick the box to release them, or choose other dates.
- Each confirmed instructor gets one message with every moved class week and answers **Still works** or **Doesn't work**. If they answer Doesn't work, they are released and the next instructor is asked.

See [Breaks, cohort holidays and merges](/help/breaks-and-holidays) for every step.

## Cohort holiday

**Cohort holiday** means a class falls on a day off set for this cohort. A public holiday of the domain or region shows **Holiday**.

1. Open the class week. The notice above the classes names the holiday.
2. Change the class's time, press **Cancel class**, or press **Keep with a reason** and then **Keep class**.

A kept class no longer shows as a problem, and your reason is saved in Activity history.

## Proposed time

An instructor can press **Propose another time** in a request to teach or a time change message. They change the date or time of any class, in the class's own time zone, and can add a note. Nothing changes until you decide, and their reply time is extended by up to 48 hours. The class week shows **Proposed time**, and Home has the task **Proposed time to check**.

1. Open the class week. Under **Proposed time**, read **Proposed times**, **Their note** and any **New problems at the proposed time**: a clash, the cohort's other classes, holidays, odd hours or unavailable dates. A clash can't be accepted.
2. Press **Accept proposed time**. The classes move for the whole cohort. Someone being asked is then confirmed. A confirmed instructor stays confirmed and is not asked again about the time they proposed.
3. Or press **Reject**, add a **Note for the instructor (optional)** and press **Reject proposed time**. They are told the time stays, and can answer their request again.

## Moved class

**Moved** means a class moved from its first time. The side panel shows **Moved** next to the class with its time before.

While a confirmed instructor has not confirmed the new time, the class week shows **Waiting to confirm a new time**. They answer **Still works**, **Doesn't work** or **Propose another time**. If they do not answer, Home shows **Time change not confirmed**. Contact them and ask them to answer the message. If they can't teach at the new time, press **Release instructor** so the next instructor is asked.

## Needs instructor

**Needs instructor** means nobody is confirmed or being asked: everyone in the order said no, did not answer, or could not be asked. Home shows **Nobody left to ask**.

1. Open the class week and choose someone under **Instructor**, then press **Assign and confirm instructor**. Or, on [Instructor requests](/requests), use **Ask a specific person** or **Change the order for this class week**.
2. If the order is too short, add instructors to it on the module's **Instructor order** tab. See [Instructors and the instructor order](/help/instructors).

**Setup incomplete** is different: something is missing, such as class times, an owner or an instructor order. The task on Home says what.

## Message failed

**Message failed** (red) means a message about this class week could not be delivered. Home shows the task **Message not delivered**, and the operations team gets an email. The class week's **Activity** shows the message as **Not delivered** with the problem.

1. Read the problem, for example a channel that no longer exists or a username that was not found.
2. Fix it. Open the instructor on [Instructors](/setup/instructors) to choose a new channel or correct the username, then press **Check Discord**. **Only show** with **Messages failing** lists everyone with a problem.
3. Open the class week, open **More options** and press **Send last message again**.
4. Press **Mark done** on the task.

If many messages fail at once, Discord or email may be down. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).

## Answer due soon

**Answer due soon** shows on a class week that is waiting for an answer, when the answer is due within 12 hours. You can change the hours under **More options** on [Automation](/settings/automation), in **How long we wait for an answer**.

Usually nothing is needed: when the time runs out, the next instructor is asked by itself. To speed things up, contact the instructor, record their answer with **Record a phone or chat confirmation**, or press **Ask the next instructor** on [Instructor requests](/requests).

## Help request

Instructors can press **Need help** in a reminder before class. They choose what they need, for example the Zoom link, or **Class materials or content**, which goes to the module owner, and how urgent it is. The class week shows **Help request open**, and the request is a task on Home for the right people.

![A help request](/help/help-request.png)

1. On Home, press **Open help request**.
2. Press **I'm on it** so the instructor and the team know someone is handling it. Use **Reassign to** to hand it over.
3. Write your answer under **Reply to the instructor** and press **Send reply**. Tick **This resolves it** when nothing more is needed. Your reply goes to their Discord channel, or a direct message, and by email.
4. If you solved it another way, write a note and press **Resolved outside the app**, or **Close (no action needed)**.

Each request has a time by which it should be answered. It depends on how urgent the instructor said it is, and is shorter when the class is close. Halfway through, the people it is for get a reminder. When the time passes, the escalation contacts are told.

When help really means "I can't teach", press **Treat as a cancellation**. The instructor is released and the app starts asking other instructors. You can also add a help request yourself, for example after a phone call: open the class week, open **More options** and use **Add a help request**.
