Settings decide when instructors are asked, how long they have to reply and which reminders go out. Settings apply to a whole domain. A cohort can change any of them for its own classes. Most teams never need to change anything.

![Automation settings](/help/automation.png)

## Change a setting

1. Open [Automation](/settings/automation).
2. Under **Settings for**, choose the whole domain or one cohort.
3. Change the values. Each field shows where its value comes from, for example **From domain settings** or **Changed for this cohort**.
4. Press **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom. For a cohort you can add a reason; it goes into Activity history.

To undo a cohort's own value, press **Use the value above**. For the domain, press **Reset to default**.

Changes apply to everything not yet sent. Nothing already sent is repeated.

## Switch reminders off

All reminders are in the **Reminders** section. You can switch any of them off:

1. **Remind the instructor while we wait for their answer**: a reminder before we ask the next instructor.
2. **Remind the instructor after they are assigned**: short reminders before the class, on the days you list (for example 10, 2).
3. **Remind the team after an instructor is assigned**: the module owners are asked if everything is ready, and the operations team is reminded about class tasks.
4. **Open tasks for the team**: repeat reminders and messages to the escalation contacts.
5. **Send each team member a daily summary**.

Next to each number, 0 means no reminder.

> Turning reminders off never stops the main automation. Instructors are still asked in order, the next instructor is still asked when there is no answer, and urgent alerts (for example a cancellation, or nobody left to ask) are always sent.

## Timing in one click

Under **Asking instructors**, choose **Standard**, **Relaxed** or **Strict**, then press **Save changes**. The exact numbers are under **More options**.

## More options

Rarely used settings are under **More options** at the bottom of the page: how long we wait for an answer, quiet hours, the default class tasks, help request targets and who gets each kind of help request.

## Message texts

1. Open [Message templates](/settings/messages) and choose a message.
2. Change the text. Details in braces, like {module} or {classes}, are filled in when the message is sent. The list under **Details you can use** shows them all.
3. Watch the **Preview**. If something required is missing, you see it in red.
4. Press **Send test message** to see it in your alert channel, then **Save changes**.
5. **Reset to default** brings back the original text.
