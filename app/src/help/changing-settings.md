Settings decide when instructors are asked, how long they have to reply, and which reminders go out. Settings apply to a whole domain, and a cohort can change any of them for its own classes. Most teams never need to change anything.

![Automation settings](/help/automation.png)

## Change a setting

1. Open [Automation](/settings/automation).
2. Under **Settings for**, choose the whole domain or one cohort.
3. Change the values. Each field shows where its value comes from, for example **From domain settings** or **Changed for this cohort**.
4. Press **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom, or **Discard**. For a cohort you can add a reason, which goes into Activity history.

To undo a cohort's own value, press **Use the value above**. For the domain, press **Reset to default**. Changes apply to everything not yet sent. Nothing already sent is repeated.

## Timing in one click

At the top, **What happens before a class** shows the days before the first class when each step happens. In the section below it, choose **Standard**, **Relaxed** or **Strict**, then press **Save changes**:

- **Standard:** start 30 days before, with a day to reply when the class is far away.
- **Relaxed:** start 35 days before and give instructors more time to reply.
- **Strict:** start 25 days before and move on to the next instructor sooner.

The exact numbers are under **More options**.

## Switch reminders off

Every reminder is in the **Reminders** section, in three parts: **While we wait for an answer**, **After an instructor is assigned** and **Open tasks for the team**. Switch any of them off, or set its number to 0. What each one does is in [Notifications and reminders](/help/notifications).

> Turning reminders off never stops the main work. Instructors are still asked in order, the next instructor is still asked when there is no answer, and urgent alerts are always sent.

## More options

Press **Show more options** for the settings most teams never change: how long we wait for an answer (including when **Answer due soon** shows), quiet hours, what happens when the last reminder before class gets no answer, class tasks, help requests and who gets each kind, planning, and how many instructors each module needs.

## Message texts

1. Open [Message templates](/settings/messages) and choose a message on the left.
2. Change the **Subject (email) or title (Discord)** and the text. Details in braces, like {module} or {classes}, are filled in when the message is sent. **Details you can use** lists them all, with **Add to the end of the message**.
3. Watch the **Preview**. If something required is missing, you see it in red. Buttons such as Yes and No are added by themselves.
4. Press **Send test message** to see it in your alert channel, then **Save changes**.

**Reset to default** brings back the original text. A message you changed shows **Changed** in the list.
