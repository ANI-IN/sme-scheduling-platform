A **task** is something the automation could not finish by itself, for example nobody left to ask, a written reply from an instructor, a message that could not be delivered, or class tasks that are overdue. Tasks are on [Home](/) under **Needs your attention**, urgent ones first.

![Tasks on Home](/help/home-tasks.png)

## Handle a task

1. Read the task. It says what happened and which class week it is about.
2. Press **I'm on it** so the team knows you are handling it. Reminders about it pause for a day.
3. Do what is needed. The buttons help: **Assign and confirm instructor**, **Open class**, **Open help request** or **Reply**.
4. Press **Mark done** when it is finished, with a short note if useful.

**Reply** sends a message to the instructor in their Discord channel and by email. Nothing about the class week changes. When a written reply really is a cancellation, press **Release and find a replacement**: the instructor is told, and the app starts asking other instructors.

## Hand a task to someone else

Choose a person under **Assign to**. They are tagged and reminded instead of you.

## Reminders and escalation

If a task stays open, the people it is for are reminded. If it is still open after a while, the escalation contacts are told and the task shows **Escalated**. Urgent tasks are reminded more often. The timing is under **Open tasks for the team** on [Automation](/settings/automation).

## Find a task

Use the filters above the list: **Type of task**, **Assigned to**, **Urgent only**, **Mine only** and **Show done in the last 7 days**. The filters only show when there are tasks. If the domain has more than one cohort, choose one at the top of Home to see only its tasks and classes.

## The cards on Home

- **Urgent tasks** and **Open tasks**, with how many were done today.
- **Classes this week**, which opens Upcoming classes.
- **Still need an instructor** in the next 30 days, which opens Instructor requests.
- **Open help requests** from instructors.

Under the tasks, **This week's classes** lists each class with its time, instructor and status.

How to fix each kind of problem is in [Fixing problems on a class week](/help/fixing-problems).
