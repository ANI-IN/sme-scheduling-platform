Open the [Schedule board](/board). Each cell is one class week of a cohort. It shows the module, its status, its instructor and anything unusual about it. Select a cell to open the class week in the side panel, where every action is.

## Views

Choose a view at the top:

- **By cohort:** one column per cohort, one row per week. Cohorts of one standard schedule are grouped together. Select the group's name to fold it away. Cohorts without a standard schedule are grouped under **No standard schedule**.
- **By instructor:** one row per instructor, one column per week. On a phone, each instructor is a heading with their class weeks as cards underneath, in date order.
- **List:** every class week in date order. On a phone, the board opens in this view.

The board opens with this week as the first row, marked **This week**. Use **Earlier**, **This week** and **Later** to move through the weeks, and choose 4, 8, 12 or 26 weeks. Past weeks are shown for reference and can't be changed here. Your view and filters are remembered on this device.

## Statuses

Each cell has one status, always shown with its colour, icon and word:

- **Confirmed:** an instructor said yes.
- **Waiting for an answer:** an instructor was asked and has not answered yet.
- **Needs instructor:** nobody is confirmed or being asked, so you choose an instructor.
- **Proposed time:** the instructor proposed another time, which you accept or reject.
- **Not asked yet:** nobody was asked yet. The cell names the Primary instructor, who is asked first.
- **Setup incomplete:** something is missing, such as class times or an instructor order, so nobody can be asked yet.
- **Paused:** requests for this class week are paused.
- **Cancelled** and **Completed**.

A week without classes during a cohort's break shows **Break** and the reason. A program break is one row across the board.

## The Legend

Press **Legend** to see every status and every badge the board, the cohort headers and the side panel can show, each with one sentence about what it means.

Badges on a cell say what is unusual, such as **Clash**, **Holiday**, **Answer due soon**, **Help request open**, **Moved**, **Extra class** or **Message failed**. Red badges need a fix now, and amber ones are worth a look. A cell shows up to three, and **+2** means there are two more inside. What each one means and how to fix it is in [Fixing problems on a class week](/help/fixing-problems).

## Needs a decision

The buttons under **Needs a decision** count every problem in the weeks shown, for example clashes, unavailable instructors, odd hours, holidays, class weeks that need an instructor and proposed times. Select one to show only the class weeks with it, and **Show everything** to see them all again. Tick **Only problems** to hide class weeks with nothing to decide.

## Cohort headers

Each cohort header shows its region (**US** or **India**), whether it follows its standard schedule (**Standard**, **Custom** with the number of changes, or **Fully custom**), how many class weeks are confirmed, for example **2 of 6 confirmed**, and **Merged into** after a merge.

**Cohort actions** in the header offers **Add a break**, **Add a cohort holiday**, **Merge with another cohort**, **Cohort settings** and **Instructor order**. See [Breaks, cohort holidays and merges](/help/breaks-and-holidays).

## The side panel

![A class week in the side panel](/help/class-drawer.png)

Select a cell to open its class week. The title names the module and the week, with the cohort underneath. From top to bottom:

- Its status and badges, for example **Instructor order** or **Chosen list** (how instructors are asked) and **At risk**.
- **Classes:** each class with its time, **Change time**, **More options for this class** and **Cancel class**.
- **Proposed time**, **Shared class** and **Standard schedule**, when they apply.
- **Instructor:** who is confirmed, with **Release instructor**, or **Assign and confirm instructor** and **Record a phone or chat confirmation**.
- **Tasks for the operations team**, and **More options** with the rest, such as **Pause requests**, **Send last message again** and **Move dates**.
- **Activity:** every message and answer for this class week.

What each action does is in [Changing a class week](/help/cancellations-and-moves).

## Change several class weeks at once

1. Press **Select class weeks**. Each cell you click is now selected instead of opened. Click it again to unselect it.
2. The bar above the board says how many are selected. Press one of its buttons.
3. Read the preview. It lists every selected class week, who is told or asked again, and every new problem the change would create. Something that **Blocks** must be fixed first. A **Warning** needs a reason, which is saved in Activity history.
4. Press the button at the bottom to save, or **Go back** to change nothing.
5. Press **Done selecting** to open cells again.

The buttons in the bar:

- **Add a break:** the break dialog opens with the cohorts of the selected class weeks, and the first selected week as its first day.
- **Move:** choose how many weeks earlier or later under **Move by**, then press **Move**. Every selected class week moves by the same number of weeks, at the same clock time.
- **Cancel:** type the **Reason**, then press **Cancel class weeks**. The people confirmed for them are told.
- **Make one shared class:** the earliest selected class week keeps the class and the others join it. You need at least two. See [Fixing problems on a class week](/help/fixing-problems).
- **Clear selection:** unticks every class week.

## Compare to standard and download

Tick **Compare to standard** to outline every class week that differs from its cohort's standard schedule. They show **Differs from the standard** or **Not in the standard**.

**Download as spreadsheet** saves the class weeks shown, with the filters you chose. The download is kept in Activity history.
