Open [Schedule board](/board). Each cell is one class week of a cohort. It shows the module, its status, its instructor and anything unusual about it. Select a cell to open the class week with all its actions.

## Views

- **By cohort:** one column per cohort, one row per week. Cohorts of one standard schedule are grouped as a track; select a track's name to fold it away.
- **By instructor:** one row per instructor, one column per week.
- **List:** every class week in date order. On a phone, the board opens in this view.

Use **Earlier**, **This week** and **Later** to move through the weeks, and choose how many weeks to show. The current week is marked. Past weeks are shown for reference and can't be changed here. Your view and filters are remembered on this device.

## Statuses

Each cell has one status, always with its colour, icon and word: **Confirmed**, **Waiting for an answer**, **Needs instructor**, **Proposed time**, **Not asked yet** (with the Primary instructor), **Setup incomplete**, **Paused**, **Cancelled** or **Completed**. A week during a break shows **Break**; a program break is one row across the board. Press **Legend** to see them all.

## Needs a decision

The counts at the top add up every problem in the weeks shown: clashes, unavailable instructors, odd hours, holidays, class weeks that need an instructor, proposed times and more. Select one to show only the class weeks with it, or tick **Only problems**. Each problem is fixed from its class week; see [When an instructor cancels or a class moves](/help/cancellations-and-moves).

## Cohort headers

Each cohort shows its time zone, whether it follows its standard schedule (**Standard**, **Custom** with the number of changes, or **Fully custom**), how many class weeks are confirmed, and **Merged into** after a merge. **Cohort actions** adds a break or a cohort holiday, merges it with another cohort, or opens its settings and instructor order.
