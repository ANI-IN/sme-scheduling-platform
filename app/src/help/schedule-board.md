Open [Schedule board](/board). Each cell is one class week of a cohort. It shows the module, its status, its instructor and anything unusual about it. Select a cell to open the class week with all its actions.

## Views

- **By cohort:** one column per cohort, one row per week. Cohorts of one standard schedule are grouped as a track; select a track's name to fold it away. Cohorts without a standard schedule are grouped under **No standard schedule**.
- **By instructor:** one row per instructor, one column per week. On a phone, each instructor's name is a heading with their class weeks as cards underneath, in date order.
- **List:** every class week in date order. On a phone, the board opens in this view.

The board opens with this week as the first row. Use **Earlier**, **This week** and **Later** to move through the weeks, and choose how many weeks to show. The current week is marked. Past weeks are shown for reference and can't be changed here. Your view and filters are remembered on this device.

## Statuses

Each cell has one status, always with its colour, icon and word: **Confirmed**, **Waiting for an answer**, **Needs instructor**, **Proposed time**, **Not asked yet** (with the Primary instructor), **Setup incomplete**, **Paused**, **Cancelled** or **Completed**. A week during a break shows **Break**; a program break is one row across the board.

## Badges and the legend

Badges on a cell say what is unusual about a class week, such as **Clash**, **Holiday**, **Answer due soon**, **Help request open**, **Moved**, **Extra class** or **Message failed**. Red badges need a fix now; amber ones are worth a look. A cell shows up to three, and **+2** means there are more inside.

Press **Legend** to see every status and every badge the board, the cohort headers and the side panel can show, each with one sentence about what it means.

## Needs a decision

The counts at the top add up every problem in the weeks shown: clashes, unavailable instructors, odd hours, holidays, class weeks that need an instructor, proposed times and more. Select one to show only the class weeks with it, or tick **Only problems**. Each problem is fixed from its class week; see [When an instructor cancels or a class moves](/help/cancellations-and-moves).

## Cohort headers

Each cohort shows its time zone, whether it follows its standard schedule (**Standard**, **Custom** with the number of changes, or **Fully custom**), how many class weeks are confirmed, and **Merged into** after a merge. **Cohort actions** adds a break or a cohort holiday, merges it with another cohort, or opens its settings and instructor order.

## Change several class weeks at once

1. Press **Select class weeks**. Each cell you click is now selected instead of opened. Click it again to unselect it.
2. The bar above the board says how many are selected. Choose what to do:
   - **Add a break:** the break dialog opens with the cohorts of the selected class weeks and the first selected week as its first day.
   - **Move:** choose how many weeks earlier or later. Every selected class week moves by the same number of weeks, at the same clock time.
   - **Cancel:** type why. Every selected class week is cancelled, and the people confirmed for them are told.
   - **Make one shared class:** the earliest selected class week hosts the class and the others join it. You need at least two.
3. Before anything is saved, one preview lists every selected class week, who is told or asked again, and every new problem the change would create. Something that **blocks** must be fixed first; a **warning** needs a reason, which is saved in Activity history.
4. Press the button at the bottom to save, or **Go back** to change nothing. Press **Done selecting** to open cells again.

## Compare to standard and download

Tick **Compare to standard** to outline every class week that differs from its cohort's standard schedule. **Download as spreadsheet** saves what is shown; the download is kept in Activity history.
