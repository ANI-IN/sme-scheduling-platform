A **cohort** is one group of students going through the modules, for example a weekend cohort and a weekday cohort. Several cohorts can run at the same time. A **class week** is the classes of one module taught by the same instructor. It starts on the day of its first class, whatever the weekday.

## Add a cohort

1. Open [Domains](/setup/domains) and open the domain.
2. Under **Cohorts and regions**, type the name under **New cohort**, for example Weekend cohort.
3. Choose the **Region**: **US Pacific time** or **India time (IST)**.
4. Choose the day it **Starts (optional)** and press **Add cohort**.

You can also create a cohort while you plan: choose **New cohort** in **Plan classes**, or plan it from a standard schedule with **Plan cohorts from a standard**. See [Standard and custom schedules](/help/standard-schedules).

## The cohort list

![Cohorts and regions in a domain's panel](/help/cohorts.png)

Each cohort under **Cohorts and regions** shows its region, how it follows its standard (**Standard**, **Custom** or **Fully custom**), and **Merged into** after a merge. Next to it you find:

- **Rename**, and the list to change its region. Changing the region does not move classes already planned. Only new classes use the new region.
- **Reset to standard**, only when the cohort is Custom.
- **Archive**: its class weeks are paused, so nobody is asked and no reminders go out until you press **Restore**.
- **Delete permanently**, which first shows what goes with it.

**Merge cohorts** under the list joins cohorts from a date. See [Breaks, cohort holidays and merges](/help/breaks-and-holidays).

## Plan classes for a cohort

![Plan classes](/help/plan-classes.png)

1. Open [Upcoming classes](/classes) and press **Plan classes**.
2. Choose the **Cohort**, or choose **New cohort**, give it a name and choose its region.
3. Choose **Start with this module**. The other modules follow in their order.
4. Under **Dates**, choose **Every week**, **Every 2 weeks** or **Paste a list of dates**.
5. Choose the **Date of the first class** (any day of the week) and the **Number of class weeks**.
6. Press **Show the plan**. You see every class with its date and time, and any warnings.
7. Change any date or time if you need to, or press **Move a week later** around a holiday. The warnings are checked again after each change.
8. Press **Create**. If there are warnings, you can still go ahead with a short reason.

When the domain has standard schedules, the dialog offers to plan a new cohort from one instead, with its breaks and class times.

The class times come from the cohort's own class times if it has them, otherwise from the module. They are local times in the cohort's region. See [US and India classes](/help/us-and-india-classes).

## Blocks, warnings and information

- **Blocks** must be fixed first, for example a module without class times or a class week that is already planned.
- **Warnings** are unusual but allowed, for example a class on a holiday, a class at odd hours, two classes of the same cohort at the same time, or an instructor with a full day. To go ahead, say why. Your reason is saved in Activity history.
- **Information** needs no reason, for example a class that is very soon.

## Add one class

For an extra, makeup or moved class:

1. On [Upcoming classes](/classes), press **Add one class**. It becomes its own class week, and instructors are asked as usual.
2. To add a class to a class week that already has an instructor, open that class week instead, open **More options** and press **Add a class to this week**. The same instructor teaches it, and a confirmed instructor is asked to confirm the new time.
3. Choose the date, **Start time**, **End time**, **Class type** and an optional **Title**, then press **Add class**.

## Upcoming classes

[Upcoming classes](/classes) lists every planned class, grouped by class week. Tick **Show past and cancelled** to see older class weeks. Select a row to open the class week.

### Find class weeks fast

Use **Search classes**, and the filters under it: **Cohort**, **Module**, **Region**, **Status** and **Instructor**.

1. Press a filter. A list opens with a box to tick for each choice. A long list has a **Search** box at the top.
2. Tick one or more choices, then press **Done**.
3. The filter shows how many choices are ticked, for example **Cohort (2)**, and each choice shows under the filters.

Choices in one filter add up, and different filters narrow down. For example, two cohorts and **Confirmed** show the confirmed class weeks of either cohort.

- **Region** shows only the classes taught in that region.
- **Status** uses the same words as the Schedule board. **At risk** shows every class week that may not have an instructor in time.
- **Instructor** matches the confirmed instructor, or the person being asked now. **No instructor yet** shows the class weeks with neither.

Select a choice under the filters to remove it, press **Clear** in a filter's list to remove only its choices, or press **Clear all** to remove every filter. Your filters are remembered for you on this device, separately for each domain, so they are still there when you come back.

Tick class weeks to change them together:

- **Choose who to ask:** tick instructors in the order they should be asked. **Use the instructor order** and **Use a chosen list** switch between the two.
- **Pause requests** and **Resume requests**.
- **Move by weeks** and **Cancel classes** open the same preview as on the Schedule board. See [The Schedule board](/help/schedule-board).
- **Clear selection** unticks them all.
