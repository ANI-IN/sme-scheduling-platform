A module is something your domain teaches, for example Concurrency. The app plans class weeks from the module's class times and asks instructors in the module's instructor order.

![The six steps of adding a module](/help/module-new.png)

1. Open [Modules](/setup/modules) and press **Add module**.
2. **Module details:** enter the name and its place in the rotation. Press **Save and continue**.
3. **Class times:** add each class of one class week. For example a live class on the first day at 9:00 AM for 240 minutes, and an assignment review 4 days after the first day at 6:00 PM for 120 minutes. The first day can be any weekday.
4. **Instructor order:** add instructors. The first is the **Primary instructor**, then **Backup 1**, **Backup 2** and so on. Drag to change the order. Use **Add new instructor** if someone is missing.
5. **Owners and operations team:** choose at least one owner. The operations team comes from the domain unless you choose people for this module.
6. **Materials:** add links that instructors get with their confirmation.
7. **Review and save:** missing items are marked. Press **Save and start using**, or save it as not in use and finish later.

## Change a module later

Open the module from [Modules](/setup/modules). Each part has its own tab.

- To give one cohort different class times or a different instructor order, open the tab and choose the cohort under **For**.
- To give one cohort its own operations team, use the **Operations team** tab.
- **Who we ask next** shows, for each class week without an instructor, who the app would ask next.

> Changes to the instructor order only affect the next person asked, never a request already sent.
