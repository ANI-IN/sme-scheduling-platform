## Sign in

1. Open the app address your team gave you.
2. Press **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose your company Google account. Personal Google accounts cannot sign in.

The first time you sign in, the app adds you to the Team page by itself, using the name on your Google account. Nobody has to create an account for you.

## Add your Discord username

The app can tag you in Discord when something needs you. It asks for your Discord username once, right after your first sign in. This is optional: without it you get email instead.

![The welcome window that asks for your Discord username](/help/first-sign-in-discord.png)

1. In Discord, find your username under your name (bottom left). It looks like maya.k.
2. Type it into the box without @ and press **Save**.

The app finds your Discord account by itself and shows **Discord found**. If you choose **Later**, you can add it any time on the [Team](/setup/team) page: open your name and type it into **Discord username**.

## When someone leaves the team

On the [Team](/setup/team) page, open the person and press **Remove access**. They can no longer sign in and are no longer tagged or reminded. Their past work stays in Activity history. You can give access back later.
