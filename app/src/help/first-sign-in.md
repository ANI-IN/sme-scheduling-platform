## Sign in

1. Open the app address your team gave you.
2. Press **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose your company Google account. Personal Google accounts cannot sign in.

The first time you sign in, the app adds you to the Team page by itself, using the name on your Google account. Nobody has to create an account for you.

## Add your Discord user ID

The app tags you in Discord when something needs you, so it needs your Discord user ID. It asks you once, right after your first sign in.

![The welcome window that asks for your Discord user ID](/help/first-sign-in-discord.png)

1. In Discord, open **User Settings** (the gear next to your name).
2. Choose **Advanced** and turn on **Developer Mode**. You only do this once.
3. Right click your own name in any server and choose **Copy User ID**.
4. Paste it into the box and press **Save**.

If you choose **Later**, you can add it any time on the [Team](/setup/team) page: open your name and paste it into **Discord user ID**.

> A Discord user ID is a long number of 17 to 20 digits. It is not your user name.

## When someone leaves the team

On the [Team](/setup/team) page, open the person and press **Remove access**. They can no longer sign in and are no longer tagged or reminded. Their past work stays in Activity history. You can give access back later.
