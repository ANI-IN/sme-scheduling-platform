title: Adding and checking instructors
summary: Add each instructor once, check their contact details, and set their preferences and days off.
order: 3
---

Each real person is added once and shared by every domain. The app never books one instructor for two classes at the same time, in any domain or cohort.

## Add an instructor

![The Instructors page](/help/instructors-list.png)

1. Open [Instructors](/setup/instructors) and press **Add instructor**.
2. Under **Basic details**, enter the full name and email.
3. If they prefer a limit, enter **Classes a month**. The app warns you before asking them for more, but you can still ask.
4. Under **Discord**, type their **Discord username**, for example maya.k (without @).
5. Press **Save**. The app finds their Discord account by itself and shows **Discord found**.
6. Under **Their channel in each domain**, choose their channel. Type part of the name to find it quickly.

They always get email too. Without a channel in a domain, they get a direct message on Discord instead. See [Discord](/help/discord).

## Check their contact details

The app only asks instructors whose contact details are checked once a domain is live.

1. Open the instructor and go to **Contact check**.
2. Press **Send contact check**. They get a short message with one button.
3. When they press the button, the badge changes to **Contact details checked**.

If you checked their details another way, for example on a call, you can mark them as checked by hand and add a note.

## Days they cannot teach

1. Open the instructor and go to **Availability**.
2. Add the first and last day they cannot teach, and an optional reason.

The app skips them for classes on those days.

## Test instructors

Test instructors are for trying things out. They never get booked in a live domain. Several test instructors can share one Discord username and use plus email addresses (for example name+1@company.com), so one person can play several instructors.

Use **Show test instructors** on the Instructors page to see them.
