## Statuses of a class week

- **Confirmed:** an instructor said yes to this class week.
- **Waiting for an answer:** an instructor was asked and has not answered yet.
- **Needs instructor:** nobody is confirmed or being asked, so you choose an instructor.
- **Proposed time:** the instructor proposed another time, which you accept or reject.
- **Not asked yet:** nobody was asked yet. The Primary instructor is asked first.
- **Setup incomplete:** something is missing, such as class times or an instructor order, so nobody can be asked yet.
- **Paused:** requests for this class week are paused, so nobody is asked.
- **Cancelled:** the class week or class will not take place.
- **Completed:** every class of the week has finished.
- **Break:** a week without classes for a cohort. Every later class week moves back by the break.

## Other words

- **Activity history:** the list of everything that happened, which nobody can change.
- **Admin:** a team member who may change who signs in, who gets messages, Discord server approvals and settings that affect everyone.
- **Alert channel:** the Discord channel of a domain where the team is tagged.
- **At risk:** a class week that may not have an instructor in time.
- **Automatic check:** the regular run, every hour, that sends messages and moves requests on.
- **Backup 1, Backup 2:** the next instructors asked after the Primary instructor.
- **Beta list:** the colleagues who may sign in while sign in is limited.
- **Chosen list:** instructors you pick by hand for one class week, asked one at a time.
- **Clash:** an instructor in two classes at the same time.
- **Class week:** the classes of one module taught by the same instructor. It starts on the day of its first class.
- **Close to the class:** the days before a class when problems are urgent (10 by default).
- **Cohort:** one group of students going through the modules, for example a weekend cohort.
- **Cohort holiday:** a day off for one cohort only. Its classes on that day show as a problem to fix.
- **Combined cohort:** the cohort two or more cohorts continue as after a merge.
- **Contact check:** an optional short message that proves an instructor's Discord and email work. Instructors are asked whether or not they are checked.
- **Custom:** a cohort planned from a standard schedule, with class weeks changed for it alone. Custom (2 changes) means two class weeks.
- **Daily summary:** one message each morning with a team member's open tasks.
- **Domain:** a program area such as Backend, with its own modules, people and settings.
- **Draft (module):** a module being set up. It is skipped when planning and nobody is asked.
- **Escalation contacts:** people told when an urgent problem is not fixed in time.
- **Fully custom:** a cohort that was not planned from a standard schedule.
- **Instructor order:** the order instructors are asked for a module.
- **Needs a decision:** the counts of problems at the top of the Schedule board.
- **No standard schedule:** the board's group of cohorts that do not follow a standard schedule.
- **Live (module):** a module whose instructors are asked.
- **Not in use:** an instructor or team member that is kept, but not used for planning or asking.
- **Ready (module):** a module with class times, an owner and at least 1 instructor in use in its instructor order.
- **Operations team:** the people who handle class tasks, failed messages and written replies.
- **Owner:** the person responsible for a module.
- **Pause all messages:** stop every message to instructors at once.
- **Practice clock:** a pretend date and time for a domain in testing.
- **Primary instructor:** the first instructor asked for a module.
- **Program break:** the same break for every cohort of a domain, shown as one row.
- **PST, PDT, IST:** US Pacific winter time, US Pacific summer time, and India time.
- **Quiet hours:** the night hours, in the class's time zone, when instructors get no messages.
- **Region:** US or India. It decides the time zone of a cohort's classes.
- **Reminder before class:** a message to a confirmed instructor before the class.
- **Setup check:** the check that a module has everything it needs before instructors are asked.
- **Shared class:** one class for several cohorts, with one instructor and one set of messages.
- **Side panel:** the panel that opens when you select a class week, with every action for it.
- **Standard schedule:** the usual plan for a cohort: its modules in order, their class times and any break weeks.
- **Task:** something a person on the team needs to do, listed on Home.
- **Test instructor:** a record for trying things out, never booked in a live domain.
- **Testing:** a domain whose messages go only to the testing channel and testing email.
- **Very close to the class:** the last days before a class, when replies are expected fastest (3 by default).
- **Warning:** something unusual but allowed. You can go ahead after you say why. The reason is kept in Activity history.
- **Welcome message:** a short message a new instructor gets once by email, and once on Discord in each domain.
