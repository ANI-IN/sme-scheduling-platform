title: Breaks and cohort holidays
summary: Give a cohort, or every cohort, a break of whole weeks, and add a day off for one cohort.

---

Breaks and cohort holidays are in [Domains](/setup/domains): open the domain and go to **Breaks and cohort holidays**.

## Add a break

A break moves every class week of a cohort from its first day on back by whole weeks. Each class keeps its local time, and the cohort's later breaks move with it.

1. Press **Add a break**.
2. Say what the break is for, choose the **First day**, the **Length** and the cohorts.
3. Check the preview: every class week that moves, its old and new week, every class with its old and new time, and who is confirmed or being asked.
4. Press **Add break**.

Nothing is saved until you press **Add break**.

## What can stop a break

- It starts in the past, or inside a class week that has started.
- A class week starts before the first day and has classes after it. Start the break on that class week's first day, or after it.
- It overlaps another break of the same cohort.
- A confirmed instructor can't teach the new dates: they would be in two classes at once, or they are unavailable then. Tick **Release** next to them to let them go from that class week and ask the next instructor, or choose other dates.

Anything unusual after the move, for example a class on a holiday or an instructor with a full day, is listed under **New problems after the move**. To go ahead, say why: your reason is saved in Activity history.

## What instructors hear

Each confirmed instructor gets one message with all their class weeks that moved, and answers **Still works** or **Doesn't work** for each class week. Someone being asked gets the request again with the new times. While messages are paused, the usual time change message for each class week goes out once they are turned back on.

## Program break

**Add a program break** gives every cohort of the domain the same break. It shows as one row, and **Remove** takes it away from every cohort.

## Remove a break

Press **Remove** next to the break. Every class week after it moves back by the break, with the same preview and checks. A break that has started can't be removed this way: change the class weeks one by one instead.

## Cohort holidays

A cohort holiday is a day off for one cohort only. Holidays for a whole domain or region are under **Holidays**.

1. Press **Add a cohort holiday**, choose the cohort and the day, and give it a name.
2. The app lists the classes of that cohort on that day.
3. Each one shows on its class week. Change its time, cancel it, or press **Keep with a reason**: your reason is saved in Activity history.

Planning a class on a cohort holiday or during a break of its cohort gives a warning.
