While a domain is in **testing**, every message goes only to your testing Discord channel and testing email. No real instructor is contacted. A yellow bar at the top of every page reminds you.

## Turn on testing

1. Open [Testing](/settings/testing).
2. Enter the **Testing Discord channel ID** and the **Testing email**. Open **How do I copy a channel ID?** if you need help.
3. Press **Save**, then **Turn on testing**.

## The practice clock

The practice clock pretends it is a different date and time, so you can see what would happen weeks ahead. Only this domain uses it. Choose a date and time and press **Set**, or use **1 hour** and **1 day** to move forward. **Real time** goes back to now.

## Run a practice class

![A practice class that passed every stage](/help/practice-class.png)

1. Make sure you have at least two test instructors with checked contact details.
2. Press **Run a practice class**.
3. The app creates a practice class week and walks through every stage: asked, reminded, moved on, confirmed, reminders before class, a help request, a time change and a cancellation.
4. You see a list with a tick or a cross for each stage. Nothing is sent and the practice class is removed afterwards.

If a stage fails, the list says what happened. Fix the setup and run it again.

## Go live

1. Check that every instructor has had their contact details checked, the instructor orders are right, and at least 8 weeks of classes are planned.
2. On [Testing](/settings/testing), press **Go live** and confirm.

From the next automatic check, real instructors get messages.
