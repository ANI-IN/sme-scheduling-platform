While a domain is in **Testing**, every message goes only to your testing Discord channel and testing email. No real instructor is contacted. A yellow bar at the top of every page reminds you.

## Turn on testing

1. Open [Testing](/settings/testing).
2. Under **Testing channel and email**, choose the **Testing Discord channel** and enter the **Testing email**. Both are needed.
3. Press **Save**, then **Turn on testing**.

The domain's status on [Domains](/setup/domains) is now **Testing**.

## The practice clock

The practice clock pretends it is a different date and time, so you can see what the automation would do weeks ahead. Only this domain uses it.

- Choose a date and a time in US Pacific time and press **Set**.
- Move forward by 1 hour or 1 day with the buttons next to it.
- **Jump to a moment before a class week**: choose the **Class week** and the moment, for example the setup check or the start of requests.
- **Real time** goes back to now.

Moving the clock also runs the automatic check. **Run the automatic check now** runs it without moving the clock, and **Last check** shows what it did.

## Run a practice class

![A practice class that passed every stage](/help/practice-class.png)

1. Make sure you have at least two test instructors. A contact check is not needed. See [Instructors and the instructor order](/help/instructors).
2. Press **Run a practice class**.
3. The app creates a practice class week and walks through every stage: asked, reminded, moved on, confirmed, reminders before class, a help request, a time change and a cancellation.
4. You see a tick or a cross for each stage. Nothing is sent, and the practice class is removed afterwards.

If it says **Some stages did not work**, the list says what happened. Fix the setup and run it again.

## Go live

1. Check that each owner reviewed the instructor order and at least 8 weeks of classes are planned. A contact check for each instructor is optional.
2. On [Testing](/settings/testing), press **Go live** and confirm.

From the next automatic check, real instructors get messages. You can switch back to testing at any time on [Domains](/setup/domains). During the beta, who may receive messages is also limited on Settings, Admin. See [Admins, the beta list and the team](/help/admins-and-beta-list).
