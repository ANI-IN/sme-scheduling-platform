## What the app does

SME Scheduling finds an instructor for every class and keeps them ready to teach. It asks instructors one at a time, reminds them, moves on when someone does not answer, and tells your team when a person needs to step in. Everything it does is written down in Activity history.

You set things up once: modules, instructors, your team and the timing. After that, the app does the asking and reminding by itself.

## The journey of a class

![The journey of a class: planned, setup check, asking instructors, confirmed, reminders before class, taught](/help/class-journey.svg)

1. **Planned.** You plan classes for a cohort on the Upcoming classes page.
2. **Setup check.** About 45 days before, the app checks the module has class times, an owner and instructors who can be asked.
3. **Asking instructors.** About 30 days before, it asks the Primary instructor. If they say no or do not reply in time, it asks Backup 1, then Backup 2, one at a time.
4. **Confirmed.** The first instructor who says Yes is confirmed. They get a confirmation with the class times and materials.
5. **Reminders before class.** The instructor gets reminders before the class and can say they are all set or ask for help.
6. **Taught.** After the class, the class week is marked as completed.

## Where to start

1. Open [Home](/) to see what needs your attention today.
2. If your domain is new, follow the setup checklist on Home.
3. Read [Setting up a new domain](/help/new-domain) for the full list of steps.

> Each class is shown in its own time zone (US Pacific or India), and US classes also show the India time. See [US and India classes](/help/us-and-india-classes). Times in Activity history are shown in your own time zone.
