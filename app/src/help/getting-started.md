SME Scheduling finds an instructor for every class week of every cohort and keeps them ready to teach. It asks instructors one at a time, reminds them, moves on when someone does not answer, and tells your team when a person needs to step in. Everything it does is saved in [Activity history](/activity).

## Sign in

1. Open the app address your team gave you.
2. Press **Sign in with Google** and choose your company Google account.
3. If the app does not let you in, ask an admin to add you to the beta list. See [Admins, the beta list and the team](/help/admins-and-beta-list).

The first time you sign in, the app adds you to the [Team](/setup/team) page with the name on your Google account. Nobody has to create an account for you.

## Add your Discord username

Right after your first sign in, a welcome window asks for your Discord username. The app uses it to tag you in team alerts and to send you direct messages. It is optional: without it you get email instead.

![The welcome window that asks for your Discord username](/help/first-sign-in-discord.png)

1. In Discord, click your picture at the bottom left. Your username is under your name, for example maya.k.
2. Type it into **Your Discord username**, without @, and press **Save**.

The app finds your Discord account by itself. If you press **Later**, add it any time on the [Team](/setup/team) page: open your name and fill in **Discord username (optional)**.

## Get started on Home

The app starts empty. Until the first setup is done, [Home](/) shows **Get started** with seven steps in order. Each step opens the page where you do it, and a finished step is crossed out.

Once you have a domain, the steps for its Discord server, modules, cohort and classes are about the domain chosen at the top of the menu, so a new domain shows the list again. Archived domains never tick a step, and neither do people who are archived or no longer have access.

1. **Add a domain:** a program area such as Backend. See [Domains and the Discord server](/help/domains-and-discord).
2. **Choose its Discord server:** in the domain's panel on Domains.
3. **Add team members:** on Team, or ask your colleagues to sign in once.
4. **Add instructors:** see [Instructors and the instructor order](/help/instructors).
5. **Add modules:** see [Setting up a module](/help/setting-up-a-module).
6. **Create a cohort:** see [Cohorts and planning classes](/help/cohorts).
7. **Plan classes:** for many cohorts at once from a standard schedule, see [Standard schedules](/help/standard-schedules).

## Finish setting up a domain

While a domain still misses something, Home shows **Set up** and the domain's name (or **Finish setting up**, once it has left Draft). Nothing is sent while a domain is a Draft. Each line links to the page where you fix it:

- Add at least one module.
- Add class times to every module.
- Put at least 1 instructor in the instructor order of every module (the number is a setting).
- Choose an owner for every module.
- Choose the operations team.
- Choose escalation contacts.
- Add the alert channel for the team.
- Turn on testing: see [Testing safely and going live](/help/testing-and-going-live).

## The journey of a class week

![The journey of a class: planned, setup check, instructors asked, confirmed, reminders before class, taught](/help/class-journey.svg)

1. **Planned.** You plan class weeks for a cohort. The Schedule board and Upcoming classes show **Not asked yet** with who is planned and the day asking starts, for example **Planned: Victor Palmer (Primary), asking starts Sat, Oct 31**.
2. **Setup check.** About 45 days before, the app checks that the module has class times, an owner and instructors who can be asked. If something is missing, the class week shows **Setup incomplete**.
3. **Instructors are asked.** About 30 days before, the app asks the Primary instructor and the class week shows **Waiting for an answer**. If they say no or do not reply in time, it asks Backup 1, then Backup 2, one at a time.
4. **Confirmed.** The first instructor who says yes is **Confirmed**. If nobody is left to ask, the class week shows **Needs instructor** and your team gets a task.
5. **Reminders before class.** The instructor gets reminders and can answer All set, Need help or Can't make it.
6. **Completed.** After the last class, the class week is **Completed**.

## Where to look each day

1. [Home](/) lists the tasks that need a person. See [Tasks on Home](/help/needs-your-attention).
2. The [Schedule board](/board) shows every cohort's class weeks and what needs a decision. See [The Schedule board](/help/schedule-board).
3. When something looks wrong, open [Fixing problems on a class week](/help/fixing-problems).

> Each class shows in its own time zone (US Pacific or India), and US classes also show the India time. See [US and India classes](/help/us-and-india-classes).
